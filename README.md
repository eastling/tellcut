# Tellcut

AI motion graphics you can edit by chat — [tellcut.app](https://tellcut.app)

## What it is

Tellcut is an AI tool for making motion graphics videos. You describe the video you want, Tellcut generates it, and then you keep refining it in plain language instead of a traditional keyframe editor. It is powered by Claude Opus 5.5 and is available on a freemium basis, so you can try it before committing.

The idea is simple: most short marketing and product videos don't need a full motion-design workflow, but they do need to be precise. Tellcut aims to give you the speed of generation with the control of editing.

## Use cases

- **App launch videos** – announce a new app or release with animated screens and text.
- **Product walkthroughs** – show a feature step by step.
- **Animated data charts** – turn numbers into moving bar, line, or pie charts.
- **Logo reveals** – short branded intros and outros.
- **Social ads** – quick, eye-catching clips for feeds and stories.
- **Explainers** – break down a concept or a process visually.

## How it works

Every frame in Tellcut is generated as code rather than as opaque pixels. That is what makes chat-based editing practical:

1. Describe the video you want in a prompt.
2. Tellcut generates the full video.
3. Select a shot on the timeline and tell Tellcut what to change — "make the title bigger", "use our brand blue", "slow down this transition".
4. Only that part changes. The rest of the video stays exactly as it was.

Each edit is saved as a version, so you can compare versions side by side and roll back if you preferred an earlier take. You never lose work by experimenting.

## Outputs

- Aspect ratios: **16:9**, **9:16**, or **1:1** — for websites, YouTube, Reels/TikTok/Shorts, and square feeds.
- Length: up to **30 seconds** per video.
- Export: **1080p MP4**.
- **Templates** to start from instead of a blank prompt.

## Who it's for

Founders, indie developers, marketers, and small teams who need polished product and marketing videos regularly but don't have a motion designer on hand — or who want to iterate on a video as quickly as they iterate on copy.

## Getting started

1. Go to [tellcut.app](https://tellcut.app).
2. Describe your video or pick a template.
3. Edit by chat until it's right, then export the MP4.
